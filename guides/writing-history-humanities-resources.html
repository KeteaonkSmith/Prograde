Flagship guide

Writing, History, and Humanities Resources

A humanities guide for students who want stronger writing, reading, research, and argument without STEM-only tunnel vision.

Who this is for

Students building evidence through writing, history, languages, philosophy, and research rather than only STEM signals.

Humanities students usually get worse recommendation lists than STEM students. This page focuses on tools, courses, competitions, and research-facing resources that help you produce better writing and clearer thinking.

How to use this page

Start with the top three.

If you want a smaller, stronger starting stack, begin with one writing tool, one reading/research source, and one visible output opportunity.

What to compare

Cost, timing, fit, and whether the effort is actually worth it.

Every entry is here because it gives a student something real: a project, a skill jump, a research path, a scholarship shot, or a clearer next step.

What not to do

Do not chase prestige without fit.

The point of this page is to save you from throwing essays at opportunities that sound good but make no sense for your stage, budget, or interests.

Best bets if you only apply to three

Start with the strongest mix of upside, realism, and payoff.
