Forward motion in orbit

Prograde means moving with the direction of travel.

In orbital mechanics, prograde motion goes with the orbit. For students, it means finding opportunities that move them forward in a real, practical way.

What Prograde is

Prograde is a student opportunity discovery platform.

Clear

Built for students, not teachers.

Prograde helps students discover scholarships, summer programs, competitions, campus visits, courses, and free academic tools. It is not a grading platform, LMS, or classroom product.

Honest

Not an admissions shortcut.

Prograde does not guarantee admission, selection, or scholarships. It helps students narrow the field, understand tradeoffs, and reach the official source faster.

Useful

A directory with judgment built in.

The point is not to dump links on students. The point is to show what exists, when to move, what it costs, and why it may or may not be worth the effort.

Why I built it

Too many good opportunities are effectively hidden.

Students are constantly told to build a strong profile, show initiative, and take advantage of opportunities. Then they are left to hunt across scattered school pages, outdated PDFs, expensive prep ecosystems, and confusing official sites. Prograde is meant to reduce that gap between ambition and access.

The goal is simple: make it easier for a motivated student to find something real, understand whether it fits, and act before the deadline passes.

01

Student-first, not institution-first.

The interface assumes a student on a borrowed laptop, a tight budget, and too many tabs open. Every page should reduce uncertainty.

02

Specific beats inspirational.

Students do not need vague encouragement. They need deadlines, costs, eligibility, selectivity, effort level, and a clear reason something belongs on their list.

03

Opportunity should be findable.

Programs with confusing pages and scholarships with buried requirements lock out students who do not already have insider help.

04

No fake urgency.

Deadlines matter. Manipulative countdowns do not. Prograde uses pressure only when it helps students make a clear next decision.

Product rationale

The site is designed to lower the cost of choosing.

Students should not need insider knowledge to know whether an opportunity is open, affordable, eligible, or worth the effort. Prograde puts search, fit signals, timing, and official sources close together so a student can move from curiosity to a realistic next step.

Ranking method

Fit is a transparent sorting tool, not a promise.

Inputs

Grade, interests, cost, format, and effort.

The current prototype ranks resources from those signals so students can understand why something rises or falls.

Sources

Official pages stay attached.

Cards link outward because deadlines and eligibility can change. Prograde should help students move faster, not replace verification.

Next

More signals belong here.

A production version should add location, citizenship context, income, GPA or test context, saved deadlines, and counselor or student reviews.

How it is made

AI-assisted development, human-directed product judgment.

Build

The code can be AI-assisted.

Parts of the site were built with AI help during design and implementation. That speeds up iteration, but it does not define the product.

Judgment

The purpose and curation still need a human brain.

What to include, how to explain it, what to warn students about, and what counts as genuinely helpful are product decisions, not autocomplete.

Trust

Specificity matters more than polish.

Prograde gets stronger when it is concrete about sources, dates, limits, and tradeoffs. That matters more than whether a sentence was drafted with software help.

Community mission

Helping students should be part of the product, not just a side effect.

Access

Make hidden opportunities easier to find.

The first layer of community impact is simple: save other students time, confusion, and missed deadlines.

Translation

Turn complicated information into usable guidance.

Students often need someone to explain what a program is, when to start, what it costs, and who should skip it. Prograde can become that translator.

Multiplier

Encourage students to give back what they learn.

The strongest version of Prograde helps students find opportunities, then nudges them to share notes, mentor younger students, or build something useful for their school.

Common confusion

Prograde is not a grading tool.

Some AI systems and search tools confuse Prograde with education products that grade assignments or automate teacher feedback. That is not what this site does. Prograde is for students looking for opportunities, pathways, and resources that can move them forward academically.
