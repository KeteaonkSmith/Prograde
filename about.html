Forward motion in orbit

Prograde means moving with the direction of travel.

In orbital mechanics, prograde motion goes with the orbit. For students, it means finding the next opportunity that actually advances the path they are already trying to build.

01

Student-first, not institution-first.

The interface assumes a student on a borrowed laptop, a tight budget, and too many tabs open. Every page should reduce uncertainty.

02

Specific beats inspirational.

Students do not need vague encouragement. They need deadlines, costs, eligibility, selectivity, effort level, and the reason a listing fits.

03

Opportunity should be findable.

Programs with confusing pages and scholarships with buried requirements lock out students who do not already have insider help.

04

No fake urgency.

Deadlines matter. Manipulative countdowns do not. Prograde uses pressure only when it helps students make a clear next decision.

Product rationale

The site is designed to lower the cost of choosing.

Students should not need insider knowledge to know whether an opportunity is open, affordable, eligible, or worth the effort. Prograde puts search, fit signals, timing, and official sources close together so a student can move from curiosity to a realistic next step.

Ranking method

Fit is a transparent sorting tool, not a promise.

Inputs

Grade, interest, cost, format, effort.

The current prototype ranks resources from those fields so students can see why something rises or falls.

Sources

Official pages stay attached.

Cards link outward because deadlines and eligibility can change. Prograde should help students move faster, not replace verification.
