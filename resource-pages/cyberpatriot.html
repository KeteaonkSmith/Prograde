What this actually feels like

CyberPatriot is closer to a season-long defense competition than a casual one-off CTF. Teams are judged on how well they secure Windows, Linux, and networking scenarios under time pressure.

What you will run into

Windows hardening, Linux hardening, network defense, team workflow

Where students usually stall

Most new teams stall because nobody owns a system, nobody practices under timed conditions, and everyone waits for a coach to explain every scoring loss.

Where to start

Start by learning the score report, then assign one machine owner per system type and practice with old images before the season gets serious.

What a good first run looks like

A good first season means learning how to read scoring feedback, finding your strongest teammate roles, and finishing rounds less panicked than you started.

What I wish I knew

Do not obsess over doing every task yourself. Teams improve faster when each person owns one slice of the image and talks constantly.
