Investor / partner summary

Prograde is a discovery engine for the opportunities students usually find too late.

Students are surrounded by scholarships, summer programs, competitions, college-credit pathways, fee waivers, certifications, campus visits, free tools, and study resources. The problem is not that these opportunities do not exist. The problem is that they are scattered, stale, hard to compare, and often discovered only by students who already have insider guidance.

ProblemAcademic opportunity discovery is fragmented and unequal.
ProductA student-first catalog that ranks resources by fit, timing, cost, and actionability.
VisionThe default map for students trying to move forward academically.

What it does

Prograde turns scattered opportunity pages into a navigable student pathway.

Discover

Find reputable resources.

Programs, scholarships, hackathons, certifications, waivers, open houses, college-credit options, internships, and free tools are indexed from official sources.

Rank

Personalize by fit.

A student profile adjusts matches by grade, goals, interests, budget, format, experience, effort tolerance, and solo/team preference.

Act

Make the next step obvious.

Cards surface cost, eligibility, status, deadlines, source links, and when to prepare, so students can move from browsing to applying.

Why now

Students need opportunity infrastructure, not another inspirational feed.

Access

Free and low-cost paths are hard to find.

Fee waivers, dual-credit options, public programs, and nonprofit resources are often buried on disconnected sites.

Timing

Deadlines arrive before students know what to search.

Many summer and scholarship opportunities close months before students start looking.

Trust

Search results are noisy.

Students must distinguish official resources from ads, stale lists, expensive camps, and content farms.

Guidance

Not every student has a counselor with time.

Prograde gives students a structured first pass before they ask adults, mentors, or teachers for help.

Data engine

The catalog can grow continuously while staying reviewed.

Scout

Automated discovery.

A recurring scout searches official and reputable sources every 12 hours for new candidate resources.

Review

Human approval before publishing.

Candidates should enter a review queue with source, eligibility, cost, deadline, category, and trust notes.

Verify

Freshness is part of the product.

Every resource should eventually carry last-checked status, next verification date, and active/closed/prep-now state.

Positioning

Prograde is not a college-admissions content site.

It is a resource discovery and action layer for students: what exists, whether it fits, when to move, what it costs, and where the official source is. The long-term opportunity is to become the trusted repository of academic access pathways, from NASA internships and AI programs to ACT waivers, Cisco NetAcad certificates, campus open houses, dual-credit options, and local scholarships.
