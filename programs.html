Student strategy

Do not confuse expensive with better.

Check cost

Free and funded programs should come first.

If a program is paid, ask what evidence it creates: research, college credit, a portfolio project, or a real supervisor.

Check fit

Match the program to your current level.

A brutal research program is not automatically better than a course or local project if you are still building foundations.

Check output

Know what you leave with.

The best summer experiences produce a paper, project, transcript, mentor relationship, paid work, or a clearer direction.
