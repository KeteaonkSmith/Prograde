Why this matters

The goal is not just to look impressive. It is to learn, contribute, and build proof that means something.

Explore

Test a field before committing to it.

A course, challenge, or visit can tell you whether you actually like the work, not just the idea of the field.

Build

Create visible evidence.

Projects, essays, papers, demos, and public work say more than “I’m interested in this.”

Share

Help the next student start faster.

Prograde is strongest when students leave behind notes, shortcuts, and context that make opportunity less hidden.

What Prograde is not

Not a grading app, not a counselor replacement, and not a promise machine.

Not grading

It is for students, not teacher workflows.

Prograde does not grade assignments, manage classrooms, or automate teacher feedback.

Not magic

Fit helps sort, not predict.

The site can help you narrow the field and keep your next step visible, but it cannot guarantee scholarships or admission.

Still human

Official sources stay attached.

Every serious decision still ends at the source page, where dates, eligibility, and rules can change.
